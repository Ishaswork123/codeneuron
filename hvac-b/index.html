COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear Answers Before You Start.

Will this replace my office staff?

No. The goal is to reduce repetitive manual work, not remove human judgment. The system should handle appropriate repeatable steps while your team remains involved where human action is needed.

We already use software. Do we need to replace it?

Not necessarily. Where practical, the workflow can be designed around the tools you already use instead of forcing your business into an entirely new system.

What if I don't know where leads are being lost?

That's exactly why the Lead Leakage Review exists. We start by mapping the customer journey and identifying where response, information collection, follow-up, or handoff may be breaking down.

Will customers know they're talking to automation?

Automation should be used intentionally. The workflow can define where automated communication is appropriate and where the conversation should be handed to a real team member.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. We handle the technical implementation and explain the workflow in business terms.

Can we start with one part of the process?

Yes. In many cases, starting with one high-friction part of the lead journey is more practical than trying to automate everything at once.
